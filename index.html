Bio-Technology Division

Tissue Culture Laboratory

Advanced Plant Tissue Culture

Using modern micropropagation techniques, we produce healthy, disease-free plantlets from carefully selected mother plants in sterile laboratory conditions. This ensures uniform growth, higher survival rates, and consistent crop quality for sustainable cultivation.

01

Stage I: Initiation & Asepsis

We source high-vitality donor cells from certified elite plants. The tissue sample (explant) undergoes thorough surface-sterilization before being placed onto a growth medium inside sterile laminar airflow cabinets.

02

Stage II: Multiplication

In plant tissue culture, the **multiplication stage** is the phase where cultured cells actively divide and increase in mass under controlled conditions. Nutrient-rich media and growth regulators help rapid cell proliferation for large-scale plant production.

03

Stage III: Shooting

Under specific chemical cues and optimized environmental parameters, the explant multiplies. This generates clusters of multiple shoots, allowing massive biological amplification in microscopic timelines.

04

Stage IV: Rooting Induction

Individual healthy shoots are isolated and introduced to a specialized root-inducing agar nutrient mixture containing auxins. This establishes strong, healthy vascular tissue arrays.

05

Stage V: Acclimatization (Hardening)

The delicate plantlets are transferred from artificial agar into organic soil mixtures. They undergo gradual environmental exposure inside state-of-the-art greenhouse hardening units to guarantee complete outdoor survival rates.

  • Currently pursuing or graduated in Biotechnology, Botany, Horticulture, Agriculture, or related sciences.
  • Basic theoretical knowledge of tissue sterilization, cell multiplication cycles, and cleanroom bio-safety controls.
  • Academic references from your biological department head.
